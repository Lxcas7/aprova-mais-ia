# PRODUCT.md: Aprova+ IA

## Product Overview
**Aprova+ IA** is a centralized public-exam intelligence workspace (*plataforma centralizada de concursos públicos*) for Brazilian *concurseiros*. It unifies three critical exam horizons in one operational tool:
1. **Ativos (Com Edital Aberto):** Public exams with active registration windows, salaries, vacancies, and exam dates.
2. **Previstos na Internet (Radar IA):** Upcoming exams tracked via AI from official gazettes (DOU/MGI), bidding waivers, union notes, and news portals, scored by a probability index.
3. **Históricos (Já Aconteceram):** Past exams with historical cutoff scores (*notas de corte*), organizing boards (*bancas*), and downloadable past papers.

Every exam is directly linked to its specific study materials (verticalized syllabus, targeted PDFs, question banks, and past papers) and a personal study tracker.

## Primary Mode
**Operate** (with **Read** clarity on exam syllabi and AI diagnoses). Users arrive in a task-oriented mindset to filter exams, verify rumor credibility, download study guides, and track completed materials.

## Target Audience & Use Scene
Brazilian civil-service candidates (*concurseiros*) studying on desktop or mobile browsers for 2 to 6 hours daily. They value high information density, scannable tabular metadata, high-contrast typography for long reading sessions, and zero visual clutter.
